Summary

Tempered is a calorie and training tracker for iPhone. It adapts your macros and workouts every week, and it logs meals with AI from a sentence or a photo.

A calorie and training tracker whose macros and workouts adapt every week, with meals logged by AI from a sentence or a photo.

What I built

  • A native iPhone app for tracking food, calories and macros, and training.

  • AI meal logging from a typed sentence or a photo.

The screens show food logging, today's calories and macros, and progress tracking.
